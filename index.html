Tech Tally

Drop in timesheet / work-order CSVs → get a performance & efficiency summary for every employee. Runs entirely offline in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

1. Add CSV files

Drag & drop CSV files here or click to choose — you can add several files at once (e.g. one per week or per crew)

2. Check the columns

Columns were matched automatically. Fix any that are wrong — only Employee name is required. Everything else just adds more metrics.
